About

I'm a Level & Combat Designer and Gameplay Programmer. I recently finished a B.Sc. in Interactive Product Design at U-Tad. I like systems that are easy to read and easy to build in-engine, and maps that make players choose and feel tension.

Most of my time goes into combat systems in Unity: data-driven abilities, enemy state machines and enemy AI. The rest goes into level design. My thesis looked at how generative AI is changing the designer's role. I keep adding new projects to this portfolio.
